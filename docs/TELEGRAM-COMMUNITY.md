# Telegram community: safe setup and rules

The community group runs on Telegram, which MindGuide AI doesn't control. These settings bring it as close as possible to the User Anonymity and Protection Framework. Members see the rules and privacy tips on the app's **/community** page before they get the link.

## 1. Group settings (do these once, as the group owner)
Open the group → tap its name → **Edit (pencil)**:

1. **Group type:** keep it **Private**, joined only through the invite link.
2. **Invite link → Request admin approval: ON.** Admins approve each request, so bots and people outside the service can be turned away.
3. **Permissions:**
   - Add members: **off** (only admins invite)
   - Pin messages, change group info: **off**
   - Send media, links and polls: **off** at first. Turn on only if you can moderate them.
   - **Slow mode:** 30 seconds, to stop spam and pile-ons
4. **Members → Hide members: ON.** Members can't browse the member list. (Telegram offers this once a group has 100 members. Until then, keep the group small and approved.)
5. **Restrict saving content: ON.** Members can't forward or save messages, which protects what people share. Screenshots are still possible, so the rules warn about this.
6. **Aggressive anti-spam: ON.** (Telegram shows this option in larger groups.)
7. **Chat history for new members: Hidden.**
8. **Admins:** at least 2 trained moderators, with only "Delete messages" and "Ban users" rights. Moderators follow the Safeguarding Policy and never give clinical advice.
9. Pin the **community rules** (below) as the first message.

## 2. Privacy for members (also shown on /community)
- Telegram shows your **name** and **username** to other members. Use a first name only or a nickname if you prefer.
- Hide your phone number: **Settings → Privacy and Security → Phone Number → Who can see my phone number: Nobody**, and **Who can find me by my number: My Contacts**.
- Profile photo: **Settings → Privacy and Security → Profile Photos → My Contacts**.
- Anything you post can be seen by everyone in the group and could be screenshotted.

## 3. Community rules (pin these)
> **Welcome to the MindGuide AI community 💜**
> This is a space for support, encouragement and learning. It is not therapy and not an emergency service.
> 1. **Be kind.** No bullying, hate, harassment, threats or shaming.
> 2. **Protect privacy.** Don't share your own or anyone else's phone number, address, workplace or private details. Don't screenshot or share others' posts.
> 3. **No medical advice.** Share experiences, not diagnoses or medication advice. Talk to your therapist for professional guidance.
> 4. **No selling or spam.** No adverts, links to other services or requests for money.
> 5. **Stay safe.** If you're in danger or thinking of harming yourself, call **112** or go to the nearest hospital now. Don't wait for a reply here.
> 6. **Moderators may remove posts or members** who break these rules. Report concerns to an admin.

## 4. Moderator escalation
If someone posts about self-harm, suicide, abuse or immediate danger:
1. Reply once, kindly, with the emergency message (call 112 / nearest hospital).
2. Hide the post from the group if it could distress others, and message the person privately only from an official admin account.
3. Report it to the Safeguarding Lead immediately, as the Safeguarding Policy describes. Don't try to counsel them yourself.
